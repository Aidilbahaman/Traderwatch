Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

TraderWatch ("we", "us") operates traderwatch.org. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account or sign in (including via Google or GitHub), we collect your email address and, where provided, your name and profile identifier. We use Supabase to manage authentication. We do not collect your Google/GitHub password — sign-in is handled by the provider.

How we use it

We use your email and name solely to create and secure your account, provide access to the member area, and send service-related messages (such as email verification). We do not sell your personal data.

Trading signals

Signals and charts are provided for informational purposes only and are not financial advice. We do not access your brokerage accounts. Any trading decisions you make are your own responsibility.

Data storage & security

Account data is stored with our authentication provider (Supabase) using industry-standard security. Session tokens are stored in your browser.

Third-party services

We use Supabase (authentication), Brevo (transactional email), and Cloudflare (hosting/DNS). Sign-in options use Google and GitHub OAuth. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us.

Contact

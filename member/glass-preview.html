Live sheet · what the app becomes

Not glassmorphism

Old trick: rgba() + blur() = frosted plastic. Liquid glass stacks four things — tinted depth gradient, backdrop-filter: blur+saturate, an inset specular rim so light catches the edge, and a grain overlay. The rim is what makes it read as a lens instead of a sticker.

Needs a light field

Glass only exists if there is something behind it to bend. The page carries four tinted sources — lime, blue, apricot, violet — at 48–70px blur. That is what you see smeared inside every card. On a flat black page it would look grey no matter how much blur you add.

Active = lit, not filled

The selected M1 pill is lime glass with a 78% white top rim and a drop glow, not a flat lime rectangle. Same for the dock icon and the logo tile. Light comes from one direction across the whole app — top-left.

Accessibility built in

Transparency Off drops blur to zero and swaps in an opaque tint — this is the real iOS Reduce Transparency behaviour, and it matters on Android where blur costs frames. Respects prefers-reduced-motion. Text contrast holds in both themes.

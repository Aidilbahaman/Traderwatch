Good to know

Before you get started.

Are the signals guaranteed to make money?

No. Signals are ideas for review, not guarantees or personal financial advice. You can lose some or all of your capital.

Is the $30 bonus automatic?

The broker sets eligibility and terms. Read its current deposit, trading-volume and withdrawal rules before registering or depositing.

Will my broker's price match the signal?

Not always. Quotes vary by provider, spread, account type and timing. Check the current price and contract details at your broker.

Is the converter rate what my broker will use?

No. It is an indicative reference-rate estimate. Banks and brokers may use different rates, spreads, fees or conversion times.

Good to know

Before you get started.

Clear expectations help you use the platform responsibly.

Are the signals guaranteed to make money?

No. Market setups are educational ideas for independent review, not guarantees or personal financial advice. Trading can result in the loss of some or all of your capital.

Will my broker's price match a setup?

Not always. Prices can differ by provider, spread, account type and timing. Verify current quotes and contract details with your own broker.

Is the currency converter rate what my broker will use?

No. Any reference conversion is indicative only. Banks and brokers may apply different rates, spreads, fees and conversion times.

Is a broker bonus automatic?

No. Eligibility and conditions are determined by the broker. Read the current deposit, trading-volume and withdrawal rules before registering or depositing.

Is Trader Watch financial advice?

No. The content is educational and informational. It does not consider your personal financial circumstances and should not be treated as investment advice or a recommendation to trade.
